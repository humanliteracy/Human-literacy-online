Tools and privacy
Record and listen back

Or record yourself. Audio stays in this page's memory; it is not uploaded or transcribed. Recording starts only after you choose consent below and press Record. Stop anytime. Each take is limited to 90 seconds.

Microphone off. Writing works without microphone consent.

Choose Play to listen. Playback never starts automatically.

Optional browser speech-to-text

This is different from local recording. Your browser may send live speech to its speech-recognition provider. We cannot control that provider's processing or retention. This does not upload a saved recording. You can decline and type instead; local recording and coaching work without it. Browser dictation may stop after a pause or return no words. Typing stops dictation to protect your edits. Each explicit dictation session is limited to 60 seconds; you can start another phrase or type.

Speech-to-text off. Nothing is sent just by opening this option.

Rehearse in your own words

Moving a draft only fills your reply. Review it there and choose Say this yourself. If a roleplay is complete, moving a draft starts a new conversation.

Privacy and how this works

Privacy: Local recording and scripted coaching use no outside service or account. Optional browser speech-to-text has a separate consent choice and may send live speech to your browser's provider. Delete, withdrawing microphone consent, closing this practice, or leaving the page discards local audio and releases its microphone. Writing is cleared by Clear private or leaving the page. Starting a new take replaces the previous local audio. Closing Tools also deletes local audio. Leaving or refreshing clears this page’s writing and conversation; nothing is saved. Your browser still controls microphone permission.
