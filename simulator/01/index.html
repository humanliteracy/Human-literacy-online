Tools and privacy
Record and listen back

Or record yourself. Audio stays in this page's memory; it is not uploaded or transcribed. Recording starts only after you choose consent below and press Record. Stop anytime. Each take is limited to 90 seconds.

Optional browser speech-to-text

This is different from local recording. Your browser may send live speech to its speech-recognition provider. We cannot control that provider's processing or retention. This does not upload a saved recording. You can decline and type instead; local recording and coaching work without it.

Speech-to-text off. Nothing is sent just by opening this option.

Rehearse in your own words

Moving a draft only fills your reply. Review it there and choose Say this yourself. If a roleplay is complete, moving a draft starts a new conversation.

Privacy and how this works

Privacy: Local recording and scripted coaching use no outside service or account. Optional browser speech-to-text has a separate consent choice and may send live speech to your browser's provider. Delete, withdrawing microphone consent, closing this practice, or leaving the page discards local audio and releases its microphone. Writing is cleared by Clear all private practice or leaving the page. Starting a new take replaces the previous local audio. Your browser still controls microphone permission.
