# TEAL human-value measurement · proposal, no collection enabled

Visibility is exposure. Usefulness, learning and humanitarian effects require different evidence. The prototype contains no analytics, forms, visitor identifiers, tracking links, or active feedback collection.

| Question | Smallest useful evidence | Interpretation / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do people understand the invitation? | After separate consent, ask 3–5 willing readers to explain TEAL, the three principles, and Human Literacy's role in their own words. Record deidentified misunderstandings, not identities. | Discovery/usability evidence from a small convenience sample, not worldwide public opinion. No comprehension score or pass rate is claimed now. |
| Can people access it? | Observe willing testers finding the letter using keyboard/screen reader, text enlargement and an older phone/slow connection. Log barrier, reproduction and resolution. | A technical automated pass alone cannot establish accessibility for every user. Fix blocking issues before wider distribution. |
| Does it help people choose learning? | With consent, observe whether a tester can find an existing lesson and start it without help. | Route usability; no claim that a click means learning or that navigation caused improvement. |
| Can a learner use a skill? | Separately approved evaluation of one chosen lesson, a novel scenario and later voluntary real-world reflection. Preserve current lesson boundaries. | Completion and quiz results are distinct from transfer. No effectiveness study or delayed contact is activated. |
| Are resource links useful? | Before an actual referral, confirm geography, eligibility, cost, accessibility and current availability with the provider; optionally ask the user whether it met the stated need. | Landing-page checks are not proof of service delivery. Do not collect health/financial details in public issues. |
| Do corrections improve the project? | A small change log: issue, source/date, fix, reviewer, verification. | Count resolved barriers/accurate corrections, not merely praise or comment volume. |
| Do future organizations report benefit? | If independently offered and separately authorized, accept deidentified methods, scope, dates, denominators, outcomes and limitations; obtain permission for any public attribution. | Self-report is not automatic causal proof or partnership. No partner or benefit exists merely because a plan names one. |
| How far does the message travel? | Later, existing platform exposure counts can be reviewed without adding site analytics if separately approved. | Impressions/followers are visibility, not food security, healthcare access, happiness, behavior change or learning. |

Proposed cadence: a short review after the first voluntary test and then only when corrections or sufficient real feedback justify it. Resource landing pages: before release, on failure reports, and every 90 days if an owner can sustain it. No recurring task or monitoring automation has been created.

If feedback research is later approved, explain purpose, voluntary choice, what minimal data is retained, where, for how long, who sees it and how deletion/correction works. Avoid sensitive stories, audio recordings, children as recruited testers, identifiable health/financial information and public spreadsheets by default. Decide retention and consent before collection; this proposal does not authorize it.

Do not use numerical targets to pressure the founder or visitors. Continue only if the evidence of usefulness justifies the time. Preserve rest, family responsibilities and financial stability.
