We won't calculate a spendable remainder until both confirmations are made. This helps prevent false reassurance.

What matters first?

Protect housing, essential utilities, food, transportation needed for work or care, insurance, and legal obligations. Risks differ by person—this tool does not assign a payment order.

Missing a payment?

Contact the provider before the due date. Ask whether they offer payment arrangements, hardship assistance, a different due date, or fee relief. Confirm terms in writing.
